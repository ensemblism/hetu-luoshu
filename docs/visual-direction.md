# Visual direction

黑曜石般的黑点、柔白点、深墨空间、少量低饱和关系色。图式是主体，数字与说明随观察主题出现。入口直接自由探索；入门文章和出处按需打开。原创音景默认关闭。

One orthographic camera serves spatial, reading and focused views. Object identity is number + local point index: 45 common points persist across diagram transitions while Hetu's ten-group fades. The morph compares positions rather than claiming a unique historical derivation.

## Contemporary teaching layers

Canonical horizontal positions and mathematical relationships stay independent of height. Reading view collapses the layout into a plane. Comparison uses the same flat scale for both diagrams.

- Hetu form/pairs: generating numbers 1–5 occupy the lower layer; completing numbers 6–10 occupy the upper layer. Five connectors show the actual pairs; heights encode generating/completing, not yin/yang.
- Luoshu form/balance: all nine number groups remain in their canonical plane. A lifted line and projection guides reveal the current three-number sum, initially 9 + 5 + 1 = 15.
- Five phases: named, hollow relationship nodes sit above the point diagram. Their ring follows the generating sequence; it does not encode compass directions. Dashed guides connect all active phase members to their relationship nodes. Generating/controlling arrows have visible direction and both endpoints remain highlighted when paused.

White and black continue to encode odd/even under every lens. Phase color is reserved for outlines, labels and links. Relationship nodes are hollow guides, separate from counted point groups. PBR lighting and a procedurally generated local room environment supply depth without fetched textures or live shadows.

The renderer draws on demand. Sphere geometry and materials are instanced; decorative ticks and projection guides are batched. DPR is capped at 1.5 on standard quality and 1 on light quality. Audio playback never drives rendering. SVG preserves phase membership, directed relationships and arithmetic when WebGL2 is unavailable.
